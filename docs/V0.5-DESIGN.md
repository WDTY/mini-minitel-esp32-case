# v0.5 complete Minitel case

v0.5 is a complete two-part enclosure for the owner's populated iodeo ESP
Minitel V2 **JST** board. Its exterior follows the project's Minitel avatar;
no geometry from the iodeo electronics enclosure is used.

## Print files

Only two current STL files are required:

1. `minitel_case_v0.5.stl` — complete Minitel cabinet, CRT front, keyboard,
   keys, vents and lower rear bay opening.
2. `minitel_esp32_bay_v0.5.stl` — removable cartridge with PCB edge guides,
   60 mm cable-turn envelope, rear USB-C opening, cable notch and flexible
   reset plunger.

The case measures approximately **78 × 123 × 74 mm**, including the open
keyboard. The bay measures **70 × 66.6 × 21 mm** at its closing flange. Both
fit comfortably on a Creality K2 build plate.

## Assembly route

1. Connect the JST plug to the unpowered PCB before putting it in the bay.
2. From the open inner end of the bay, feed the broad USB-C edge into the two
   flared edge guides and slide the board toward the bay's rear face.
3. Lay the cable in a relaxed U: forward from the JST, around the open front of
   the bay and back along the right side. Place it in the open-top rear notch.
   The DIN plug never has to pass through a printed hole.
4. Check that USB-C is centred in its rear opening and that the protruding
   printed reset pad moves freely.
5. Slide the complete loaded bay into the opening at the lower rear of the
   Minitel until its flange meets the cabinet. The shallow side beads provide
   light retention.

The PCB rests only in 1.0 mm edge channels. There is no snap-on board plate.

## Measured inputs and clearances

| Item | Value |
|---|---:|
| Gerber PCB envelope | 47.105 × 36.218 mm |
| Owner's measured PCB envelope | approximately 46.9 × 36.1 mm |
| Populated height | 10.4 mm |
| PCB thickness | 1.6 mm nominal |
| PCB slot height | 2.2 mm |
| Vertical PCB clearance | 0.30 mm per side |
| Horizontal guide clearance | 0.35 mm per side |
| Case/bay sliding clearance | 0.40 mm per side |
| USB outer edge to cable-turn front | 60 mm |

The cable reference is 5 mm in diameter because the cable itself has not been
measured. Its route and exit are open, so the printed part does not clamp the
cable.

## Creality K2 baseline

- 0.4 mm nozzle and fixed 0.20 mm layers.
- PLA for the first print.
- Print both parts in their exported orientation.
- 3 walls, 5 top/bottom layers and 12–15% gyroid infill.
- No generated supports. The case uses 45° internal bay shoulders and limits
  its final internal bridge to 30 mm. Use normal bridge detection and full
  bridge cooling.
- Do not scale the models and leave XY/hole compensation at zero for the first
  fit test.
- A 4–6 mm brim is optional for the case if the keyboard's front corners lift.

## Multicolour treatment

STL does not store filament colours. Import `minitel_case_v0.5.stl` as one
object and use surface painting in Creality Print or OrcaSlicer:

- cabinet and keyboard deck: warm beige;
- CRT face: near-black green;
- normal keys and spacebar: dark grey-brown;
- prompt, cursor, Enter key and status lens: bright green.

All coloured faces are raised or bounded so surface-fill painting can select
them without extra colour STL files. The bay can use the cabinet colour or a
slightly darker beige.

## Hidden watermark

Both print parts contain an enclosed `WDTY v0.5` void. The void is 0.60 mm
high at Z 0.60–1.20 mm: exactly three layers with the 0.20 mm K2 baseline. It
is invisible from outside and can be found in the slicer's layer preview.
Adaptive layer height changes the layer count, so keep the first 1.4 mm at
0.20 mm when preservation of the three-layer mark matters.

## CAD verification

- Both final STL files are closed, with zero nonmanifold edges.
- The exact Gerber PCB and conservative component envelopes are sampled at 49
  positions through the PCB-to-bay insertion route.
- The complete loaded bay is sampled at 71 positions through the rear case
  opening. The shallow retention beads are intentionally excluded from the
  hard-collision check because they provide the designed friction contact.
- The routed PCB/component/cable envelopes do not intersect the rigid bay.
- The reset plunger deliberately reaches the switch envelope. Physical switch
  travel remains a first-print check because the photos do not provide exact
  switch-body dimensions.

Rebuild and check from the repository root:

```sh
openscad --export-format binstl -D 'part="case"' -o stl/v0.5/minitel_case_v0.5.stl src/minitel_v05.scad
openscad --export-format binstl -D 'part="bay"' -o stl/v0.5/minitel_esp32_bay_v0.5.stl src/minitel_v05.scad
python3 tools/check_v05_clearance.py
python3 tools/review_v05.py
```

Renders demonstrate the modelled route; the first physical print remains the
authority for filament shrinkage, cable stiffness and tactile-button travel.
