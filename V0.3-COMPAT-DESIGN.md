# v0.3 compatibility design

v0.3 is a corrective, test-first upgrade for an already printed v0.2 body.
It deliberately leaves `minitel_body_v0.2.stl` unchanged.

## Design approach

- A separate open carrier receives the four legacy v0.2 locating pins.
- The PCB is held by its outer edges instead of the mismatched drill holes.
- The carrier shifts the PCB approximately 10 mm right while keeping it within
  the depth range of the existing side opening, so
  USB-C and RESET can approach the existing right-side openings.
- The front is split into a shallow inset lid and a separately printed
  keyboard. Both parts can be oriented with broad, flat faces on the build
  plate and should need substantially less support than `minitel_front_v0.2`.
- A separate reset plunger has a 4.8 mm visible cap, 2.2 mm shaft and shallow
  3.35 mm retaining collar. The cap is intended to project about 1 mm beyond
  the body.

## Test order

1. Print only `minitel_carrier_v0.3-test.stl`.
   Rotate it so its broad orange frame plane lies flat on the build plate.
2. Slide it over the four existing v0.2 pins without forcing it.
3. Check the empty edge cradle before inserting the PCB.
4. Insert the PCB edge-first and verify USB-C, RESET and JST routing.
5. Test the reset plunger manually; shorten its inner tip if necessary.
6. Only after the electronics fit, print the lid and keyboard.

## Prototype warning

This is a dimensional recovery prototype based on the v0.2 shell, the official
Gerber envelope and owner-supplied physical-fit photographs. Do not glue parts
or remove the legacy posts during the first test. Report tight sockets, carrier
interference and connector offsets in millimetres before printing the lid.
