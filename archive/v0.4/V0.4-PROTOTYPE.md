# v0.4 — rear-entry assessment prototype

This is a geometry-review prototype, not a certified fit or support-free print.
It replaces neither the published v0.3 files nor the user's printed v0.2 body.
The new one-piece outer body requires a new print if adopted.

## Architecture

The official iodeo box is rotated 180 degrees about Z and translated by
[23.75, 58, 10] mm. No scaling, rail edits, or mesh deformation is applied.
The cassette is horizontal, with its end cap at the lower rear. The Minitel
front and keyboard are integrated into the body; there is no large front lid.
An 0.2 mm overlap with the donor's lowest exterior skin connects the box to
the outer floor. The original cap is exported separately in assembly coordinates.
Its opening is at the rear, not the bottom.

The cable slot connects to the rear access opening. It is intentionally loose
and has no validated strain relief. Cable routing above/alongside the cassette,
JST plug clearance through the donor's circular extension, PCB insertion, USB
plug reach, and reset operation all require the owner's physical donor test.
No populated PCB model is supplied: previous PCB placement renders were only
hypotheses, not an established mating transform for this donor.

## Files

- `stl/minitel_body_v0.4-review.stl`: integrated outer body + donor box.
- `stl/minitel_cover_v0.4-review.stl`: rigidly positioned original cap.
- `stl/minitel_assembly_v0.4-review.stl`: visual assembly, NOT a print job.
- The existing v0.2 screen and accent meshes remain cosmetic inserts.

All exports use mm. In the slicer, lay the separate cap on its broad face and
place it on the bed. The body has an enclosed roof and horizontal internal
surfaces; support and its removal must be reviewed. Do not print the full body
until accessibility of required supports has been checked. The rear opening
is intentionally oversized for assessment, not a finished dust-tight closure.

## Verification results

- Body: 22,930 triangles, one connected component, zero nonmanifold edges.
- Cap: 1,064 triangles, one connected component, zero nonmanifold edges.
- Assembly: 24,558 triangles, three connected components, zero nonmanifold edges.
- Body bounds: 68 x 101.6 x 60 mm, including the projecting keyboard.
- Donor input SHA-256 hashes equal the upstream copies byte for byte.
- Cap positions sampled every 1 mm over 20 mm rearward travel have empty
  intersection with the added outer body. This is a sampled test, not continuous
  collision certification.
- The same test against the complete body finds six small interference regions
  at the ORIGINAL donor interface (x +/-19.75, y 59.2..60.2, z 5.3..14.8 mm).
  This may reflect snap-fit retention, but is not confirmed. The cap was not
  modified to hide these overlaps. Await the owner's original-case print test.
- Preview PNGs are software renders of the exported meshes. No PCB or cable
  mesh is presented as a verified installed assembly.

## Rebuild

From repository root:

```sh
openscad --export-format binstl -D 'part="body"' -o archive/v0.4/stl/minitel_body_v0.4-review.stl archive/v0.4/src/minitel_v04.scad
openscad --export-format binstl -D 'part="cover"' -o archive/v0.4/stl/minitel_cover_v0.4-review.stl archive/v0.4/src/minitel_v04.scad
openscad --export-format binstl -D 'part="assembly"' -o archive/v0.4/stl/minitel_assembly_v0.4-review.stl archive/v0.4/src/minitel_v04.scad
```
