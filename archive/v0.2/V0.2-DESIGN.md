# v0.2 proportion study

> Physical-test update: the PCB locating pins and USB-C position do not align
> reliably with the real JST-board. Do not force the PCB or print another v0.2
> body. The corrective design should preserve the printed shell by using a
> separate internal carrier and a shallow inset/snap front.

v0.1 proved the PCB mounting concept, but its `60 x 60 x 34 mm` cabinet and
13 mm keyboard projection read more like an arcade cabinet than an open
Minitel.

The v0.2 work-in-progress uses a new envelope:

| Feature | v0.1 | v0.2 WIP |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Cabinet width | 60 mm | 68 mm |
| Cabinet height | 60 mm | 60 mm |
| Cabinet depth | 34 mm | 58 mm |
| Keyboard projection | 13 mm | 38 mm |
| Screen insert | 41 x 22.2 mm | 51.2 x 28.6 mm |

The target is derived independently from owner-supplied photographs and the
published approximate real-machine envelope of `250 x 220 x 260 mm`. The
downloadable Sketchfab reference model was not imported or reused because its
page carries a NoAI restriction.

Research references:

- <https://hackaday.com/2022/02/16/teardown-alcatel-telic-1-minitel-terminal/>
- <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/minitel-1982-france-864f54ce4e1f41abab0688b88a45babf>
- <https://minitel.us/terminals>

v0.2 was published as a test prototype. Its fit-critical PCB coordinates,
USB-C opening, RESET access and JST cable exit were based on the official iodeo
v2.2 Gerber/drill files and photographs, but the physical test demonstrated
that the coordinate transformation was not reliable enough for the real board.
