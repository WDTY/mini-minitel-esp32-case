# Contributing

Fit reports, photographs of completed prints, slicer profiles and OpenSCAD
improvements are welcome.

When reporting a fit issue, please include:

- board revision and connector variant;
- printer, nozzle size, material and layer height;
- measured PCB dimensions when available;
- the exact part and location that needs more or less clearance;
- a photograph with a ruler or caliper, avoiding personal information in the
  background.

Please keep derived designs compatible with the repository's CC BY-SA 4.0
license and retain the upstream iodeo attribution.
